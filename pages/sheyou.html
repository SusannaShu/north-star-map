SHEYOU

Rent fashion. Reduce waste.

A peer-to-peer fashion rental and resale marketplace. One dress, ten lives. Designed, built, and shipped solo — React front end, Strapi back end, Stripe payments.

try it live

Browse & discover

A masonry grid under a single filter bar — transaction type, category, size, fulfillment. Filter state syncs to the URL (?forRent=true), so every filtered view is shareable and back-button safe.

Rent, not just buy

Buy and Rent are two tabs of one product page. Rental period, refundable deposit, and pickup vs. shipping are all resolved before the cart — the cart only confirms decisions, it never asks new questions.

Checkout without surprises

From pickup-filtered browse to paid order in four decisions. Local pickup is a first-class fulfillment method — distance-aware, confirmed with a pickup code at handoff — and saved cards make confirmation a single tap.

Transactions & payouts

Both sides of the marketplace live in one dashboard: orders move through Paid → Coordinating Pickup → Complete, while the wallet tracks pending earnings and Stripe-powered payouts.

Digital licensing — watermark to clean image

Try-on previews are watermarked. Purchase a one-time digital license and the watermark is removed instantly — clean full-res image, downloadable, shareable, yours forever.

Every screen size

Same components, one breakpoint system. The grid drops to two columns, filters stack, nav collapses into a full-width sheet, and the product gallery becomes swipeable.
