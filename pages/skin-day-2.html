NORTH STAR / BUILD SCHEDULE / DAY 2

Day 2: seal + flanges

TPU seal setting + flanges on the luer-barbs + test pouch. Unlocks: a sealed pouch unlocks pressure; flanges unlock the bladder.

about 1.75 h · 3 steps
← Day 1the pathDay 3 →

Step by step

STEP 1

Find the seal setting

TPU face to face, PTFE both sides, steam off. Strips at silk / wool / cotton, 10 s firm, cool under a book, then peel

STEP 2

Weld TPU flanges onto the luer-barbs

Cut a 20 mm TPU disc, hole a touch smaller than the fitting's neck; push the barb through; PTFE both sides, iron around the neck at the seal setting from step 1, 10 s, cool under a book. Make 4 (3 used + 1 spare)

STEP 3

Seal a test pouch around one flanged luer-barb

8 x 8 cm; weld the flange into one panel, then close the seams; barb unions / tees only where a joint needs them

Done when

A seam that tears before it peels; 4 flanges that do not lift off the neck; one sealed 8 x 8 cm pouch cooling under a book
