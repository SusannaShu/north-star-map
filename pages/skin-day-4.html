Day 4: rail + heater

5 V rail + heater chain on a tile, held at 36 C. Unlocks: a steady 36 C heater unlocks warmth.

about 2 h · 3 steps
← Day 3the pathDay 5 →

Step by step

STEP 1

Rail: USB-C, 3 A fuse, bus

Supply to the screw terminal, inline 3 A fuse, red/black bus; 1000 uF across the DRV8833 supply; meter 5 V

STEP 2

Heater chain on a tile

MOSFET (LR7843), KSD301 in series, film; DS18B20 taped beside. Not on the bladder. MOSFET drop under 0.1 V at 1.6 A

STEP 3

Firmware loop: 36 C, cap 40 C

1 s time-proportioned, one film at a time; log 20 min while you cut the bladder panels

Done when

5 V at the terminals; MOSFET drop under 0.1 V at 1.6 A; film holds 36 +/- 1 C for 10 min, with the cutoff proven on Day 1
