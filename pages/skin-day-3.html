Day 3: leak + pressure

Leak test the pouch + pressure on the pouch. Unlocks: a pressure trace unlocks listening to a hand.

about 1.5 h · 4 steps
← Day 2the pathDay 4 →

Step by step

STEP 1

Leak test: water + syringe squeeze

Distilled water in through the flanged luer-barb; squeeze 1 min over a paper towel, flange and seams both

STEP 2

Wire the MicroPressure

Qwiic cable to the WROOM I2C (SDA GPIO21 / SCL GPIO22), address 0x18; log at 25 Hz

STEP 3

Air pocket on the pouch

Pouch's flanged luer-barb, a barb union, then the 2 mm tube stretched over the barb and up to the sensor; keep 2-3 cm of air so the sensor never gets wet

STEP 4

Log press, squeeze, hold

3 reps each; label the trace

Done when

Towel dry after a 1 min squeeze; press, squeeze and hold look different on the pressure trace
