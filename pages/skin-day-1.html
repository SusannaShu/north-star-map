Day 1: touch + cutoff

Touch on scrap + 4 temperature probes + prove the KSD301 40 C cutoff. Unlocks: a proven 40 C cutoff unlocks the heater.

about 1.75 h · 3 steps
the pathDay 2 →

Step by step

STEP 1

Touch on a copper-nickel scrap

3 strips 2 x 10 cm under felt + organic fabric, leads to ESP32-S3 touch pins GPIO5/6/7 (native touchRead; the MPR121 is only a backup). Stroke both ways, tap, hover 2 cm

STEP 2

DS18B20 bus on the ESP32

All 4 probes on GPIO41, one 4.7 kOhm pull-up to 3.3 V; print each address + C once a second

STEP 3

Prove the KSD301 cutoff

KSD301 in a zip bag in a mug of hot water with a DS18B20 beside it, meter on continuity: write the temperature where it opens and where it closes again as it cools. Do 2 of the 5

Done when

3 strips fire in order on a stroke; 4 probes read within 0.5 C of each other; the KSD301 opens near 40 C and closes again as it cools
